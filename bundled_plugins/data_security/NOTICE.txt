The local capability risk categories and candidate-pattern approach are adapted
from AI-Infra-Guard (Apache-2.0), commit 30f421257c3d1f0ce0a50b32e8f75dc0a3180773.
Local adaptation: bounded, advisory static checks; no model-based audit runtime.
Matches are audit hints, not proof of malicious execution or data exfiltration.
